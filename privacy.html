Privacy Policy — BillBeam

Last updated: November 18, 2026

BillBeam is designed so that your data never leaves your iPhone.

What BillBeam collects

Nothing. BillBeam has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server that receives your data.

Where your receipts live

Receipts you scan or import — including their photos, amounts, stores, dates and categories — are stored only in the App's storage on your own device. They are included in your own iPhone/iCloud backups only if you enable those in iOS; Apple processes those backups under Apple's privacy policy, and we have no access to them.

On-device processing

All scanning, text recognition (OCR), categorization and the in-app assistant run on your device. Your receipt contents are never uploaded for processing.

Store logos

To show a store's logo, the App requests the icon from that store's public website or a public icon service, sending only the store's domain name (for example, "walmart.com") — never your receipts, amounts, dates or any personal information. If you are offline, a simple monogram is shown instead.

Purchases

BillBeam Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

BillBeam is a general-purpose finance organization tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
