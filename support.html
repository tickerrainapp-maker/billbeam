Support

Questions, problems or suggestions — write to tickerrain.app@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Where are my receipts stored?

Only on your iPhone. BillBeam has no server and no accounts, so nobody — including us — can see or recover your receipts. If you move to a new iPhone, your receipts travel with your device transfer or iCloud Backup restore, like the rest of your apps' data.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Pro tab in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple account, so it also follows you to a new iPhone automatically.

How do I cancel BillBeam Pro?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BillBeam → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How does the Pro guarantee work?

During a paid Pro year, the app adds up the value of the receipts its return and warranty alerts covered in time — you can watch the counter in the Pro tab. If that total ends up lower than the price you paid for the year, the app shows you an App Store offer code for the following year of Pro, free. See the Terms of Use for the full rule.

The app misread one of my receipts

Faded or crumpled paper can fool any reader. Open the receipt, check the amounts against the paper, and edit anything that is off — BillBeam flags reads it is unsure about in the "To Review" list so you can correct them. A flat receipt, good light and the flash on in dark places give the best reads.
